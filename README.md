# Lifeline Fitness

A single-file fitness tracker. Open `index.html` in a browser.

Tracks daily healthy/fast food, meals, water (5 L goal), steps (11,000 goal), weight, tea and coffee, and a gym log with a rest timer. Includes week, month and year views, and PDF or Gmail reports.

Data is saved on your device (or to your Claude account when run inside Claude).
